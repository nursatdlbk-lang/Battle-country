# Third-party assets

## flag-icons 7.3.2

The 32 files in `public/assets/countries/*/flag.svg` come from the pinned
[`lipis/flag-icons` v7.3.2 release](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/v7.3.2/flags/4x3).
They are stored locally so preview and Remotion rendering do not require a network connection.
